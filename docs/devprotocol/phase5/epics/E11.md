## 1. Epic goal

Record the gap, and — since 2026-08-27 — the decision that closed it. This epic names Cart, Order,
Delivery, Payment (`phase2/BOUNDED_CONTEXT.md:190`) and used to list what blocked starting design; it now
records that design never starts, [`ADR-038`](../../phase3/adr/ADR-038-commerce-is-permanently-out-of-scope.md).
It ships no collection, no migration, no model, no resolver, no schema builder, no state machine, no
sequence diagram of a checkout flow, and it never will. Per `phase5/CONSTRAINTS.md` §6: "a risk-register row
naming the gap is fine; a story designing the fix is not."

⚠️ **The four are named here so they can be refused, not so they can be built.** Their presence in this
file, in BC-11, in `UBIQUITOUS_LANGUAGE.md` §18 and on the event-storming board is vocabulary, not a
backlog. Re-opening this needs an ADR superseding ADR-038 — taken by the platform owner — and nothing
short of that: not a story, not a "first small step", not one `price` field on the grounds that it is
only one field.

### E11-S01 — Record the commerce gap and its blocking questions   `built 2026-08-13`
Technical story. Keep the platform's one authoritative record of what Ordering & Fulfilment would need
before design can start, so no future agent infers a shape from the other 10 contexts and builds ahead
of a decision nobody made.
**domains:** documentation
**Acceptance criteria:**
- `phase2/BOUNDED_CONTEXT.md` §BC-11 and this file agree on the same 4 named concepts (Cart, Order,
  Delivery, Payment) — no 5th concept invented, no concept dropped.
- Every blocking question below is traceable to a real open question in an upstream Phase 1/2 document,
  not invented in this file.
**Traces:** BCON entries n/a (no gate applies to undesigned code); `phase5/CONSTRAINTS.md` §6.
**Evidence:** absence itself — see §3 above for the grep/file-listing that proves it.

**Built 2026-08-13.** Nothing was designed and nothing was invented — the story is the record, and building
it means re-running the checks it rests on and repairing what had drifted. **Criterion 1 holds**: BC-11
(`phase2/BOUNDED_CONTEXT.md:217-222`) and this file name the same four concepts, Cart / Order / Delivery /
Payment, with no fifth and none dropped. One defect was found on that comparison and fixed: BC-11 quoted
`item.js` as saying a price "would be a guess at a design decision nobody has made", which is not a string in
that file — it now quotes `item.js:11-13` exactly. §3's evidence was re-run rather than trusted: zero
commerce mutations across the nine services, `item` still has no `price`, and the schemas directory listing
was stale by three entries, none of them commerce.

⚠️ **Criterion 2 holds for four of the five questions, and question 4 is the exception — deliberately kept.** (Read as history: all five closed on 2026-08-27, E11-S02.)
Questions 1 and 3 trace to `phase2/BOUNDED_CONTEXT.md` §7 open questions 4 and 5, question 2 to `item.js`'s
own comment, question 5 to `phase1/NFR.md` open question 1. Question 4 — whether order-adjacent operator work
needs a tier of its own — says outright that it is raised nowhere upstream, and it stays: deleting it to
satisfy the criterion would remove a real blocker, and it is a question about tier topology (ADR-002,
`phase2/UBIQUITOUS_LANGUAGE.md` §Service pair) rather than a commerce design decision, so recording it
invents no shape. The criterion is what caught it, which is the criterion working.

**What `built` means here, since the epic's title says WILL NOT BUILD:** the record is current and
verified, not that Ordering & Fulfilment exists. No collection, no migration, no model, no resolver, no state

**Built 2026-08-27.** ADR-038 is the record; this story is what carried it through the corpus. Two findings

it is the one route to a `price` field that ADR-038 would not otherwise have blocked.
